Session Goals

  • Apply required resident modifiers consistently
  • Document what each visit type requires
  • Operationalize logistics patients actually ask about
  • Close with clear plans, timelines, and backup plans

High Value Habits

  • Default to level 4 when appropriate, use level 2 when clinically indicated
  • Add GC to all resident encounters, G2211 for longitudinal care
  • List orders today vs due next clearly before patient leaves
  • Translate advice into ounces, stores, locations, and next steps

🔍 Visit Type Decision Tree

Step 1: Has patient been seen by your group/specialty in past 3 years?
❌ NO → New Patient (99203-99205) | ✅ YES → Established (99212-99215)
Step 2: Is this preventive care only?
✅ YES → Annual Physical or AWV | ❌ NO → Problem-focused visit
Step 3: Recent hospitalization?
✅ YES → Consider TCM codes (99495/99496) | ❌ NO → Standard E&M codes

📋 Visit Types and Coding

Visit Type Definition and Notes Typical CPT Modifier Add-on
New Patient New to the clinic. Longer intake. Avg. complexity often maps to level 4. 99203-99205 GC G2211 (if applicable)
Established Visit Routine follow up, new complaint, or same day issue. 99212-99215 GC G2211 (for continuous care)
Annual Physical Preventive only. If problem management is addressed, add problem visit. Age/payer specific codes GC None by default
Medicare AWV Screening, cognition, function, SDOH, safety. No hands-on exam. AWV codes per CMS GC G2211 with modifier 25 (2025+)

Documentation Focus

New Patient Intake

  • Medical, surgical, family, and social history
  • SDOH (affordability, transport, safety, pharmacy access)
  • Chief concern, exam, orders, plan, and backup plan

Established Visit Structure

  • IPO frame: Input, Process, Output
  • Output includes orders today, items for next visit, timing

⚖️ Assessment & Plan

  • What you see, what you think, what you will do now
  • Contingency if the plan does not work
  • Less emphasis on ROS/HPI, more on precise A&P
  • Document when you will recheck and how success will be assessed
💡 Pro Tip
Patients gain confidence when plan B is explicit. Always include the contingency in your plan.

Post-Hospital Follow Up Essentials

Timing Window
Seven vs fourteen days affects code selection and reimbursement (rates vary by location)
Why and Where
Hospital name, dates, admitting diagnosis, key events
Med Rec ⚠️
Show current active meds after changes. Note is incomplete without it.
Pendings
Outstanding studies, referrals, home services, durable equipment
Ownership
Route to navigation hub or case management for access/social barriers
